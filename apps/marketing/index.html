Release Lab Blog

An experimental blog product. It exists to test how one release tag ships several apps at once.

What you get

  • A public blog where readers read posts and leave comments.
  • A staff app, in the browser or on the desktop, to write and edit posts.
  • A mobile app to read posts and comment on the go.

Every app reads the same API, and every release ships them together under one version number.

This is an experiment. It is not a real product and is not for sale.
